Know where your money goes, and plan where it should go next.

The Personal Finance Advisor Bot records your income and expenses, builds a budget that fits your spending, and sends you a monthly report. AI analysis turns your numbers into clear saving suggestions.

Try a budget plan

Enter one month of income and spending. The demo uses simple budgeting rules in your browser, and nothing is sent anywhere. The full project sends the same data to the Gemini API for deeper analysis.

How it works

The bot is built with Flask. It receives your input, passes it to the Gemini API, and returns three results. API keys stay on the server.

  1. Record. Add your monthly income and log expenses by category.

Features

Income and expense tracking

Log daily and weekly spending by category in one place.

Personalised budget plans

Get category limits based on your income and your own spending.

Saving suggestions

See which categories are over budget and what to cut first.

Monthly summary report

Review income vs. expenses, savings achieved, and next month's goals.

Saving progress

Follow how close you are to your goal each month.

Secure by design

API keys are managed on the server and never exposed to the browser.

Planned next

Predictive spending analytics, investment suggestions, goal-based savings tracking, and financial health monitoring.

Who it helps

Salaried professional

Logs rent, food, transport and entertainment. Gets overspending flagged, a budget plan, and a monthly report with goals for next month.

College student

Sets realistic limits on a small allowance and gets advice on cutting unnecessary spending.

Freelancer

Logs income from several clients. Budgets adapt to changing income, low-saving months are highlighted, and the bot suggests ways to build an emergency fund.

Household manager

Tracks shared income across groceries, utilities, education and healthcare, and sees where the family overspends.
